# The creator brief: brief.md

What a creator needs to make posts that get paid, in the order they need it. Write it plainly: short lines, real
numbers, no hype. Step 2 (tiktok-ftc-check) adds a "Before you post" checklist at the top and writes
`brief.checked.md`; only that version goes to creators.

```markdown
# <Brand>: <platform> <format> campaign: creator brief

## The campaign in one minute
What the product is (from its store page), what you post, and what you can earn. Who runs it and where to join.

## Pay
The table from `campaign.py pay`, then what it means: the cap, the views needed to qualify, how many capped posts the
budget covers, and that posts made after the budget is spent earn nothing (with budget_left if known).

## The rules
Platform, format (native photo mode, not a video), slides per post, language, the audience share, quality, no reposts.
Quote the campaign where wording matters.

## Your account
Your own account, under your own name, in your own country. A Tier-1 audience comes from being in a Tier-1 country.
What the bio says. Turn on the content disclosure setting for every campaign post.

## What to post
For each format (2 to 4), from the slideshow-content research:
- **The evidence.** How many winning posts, from how many creators, saves per 1,000 plays, with links.
- **Who can post it.** Anyone, or only creators with the experience it describes. A first-person story is only for
  someone it happened to.
- **The slides.** Slide by slide: the hook slide's words, the middle slides, where the product appears, the last slide.
- **Hooks.** Three to five, in the winners' pattern.
- **The caption.** A template, starting with the disclosure.
- **The pictures.** What each slide shows and where it comes from: your own photos, the product's real screenshots,
  or licensed photos.

## Making the slides
Size (1080x1440, 3:4), fonts and text size, how many words a slide holds, and how to post them in TikTok's photo mode.
Link to the example post if one was rendered (out/contact.jpg).

## Posting
The steps in the TikTok app, in order, including the disclosure setting, the sound, and the time of day if the
research shows one.

## Submitting and getting paid
Where, how soon after posting, what proof (the analytics screenshot and what it must show), and when payouts happen.

## Held for the compliance check
The campaign's requirements this brief doesn't turn into steps yet, quoted exactly, each with the rule it may break.
Step 2 rules on them.
```
